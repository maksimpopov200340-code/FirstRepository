# Week 1 Training Plan

## Athlete and goals

- Height: 175 cm
- Weight: 62 kg
- Current 10 km time: approximately 50 minutes
- Long-term target: 10 km in 40 minutes
- Basketball goals: improve midrange and three-point shooting after dribbling, improve 1-on-1 play, stamina, and physical strength
- Facilities: gym and basketball court
- Week 1 objective: adapt gradually and finish each workout feeling capable of doing more

## Available training time

- Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday: maximum 40 minutes
- Wednesday, Saturday, Sunday: maximum 90 minutes

## Wednesday — Basketball

**Total time:** 70–80 minutes

### Warm-up — 10 minutes

- Light jogging
- Lateral movement
- Dynamic ankle and hip mobility
- Low-intensity dribbling with both hands

### Shooting — 40 minutes

1. Close-range form shooting — 20 makes.
2. Stationary midrange — 5 positions × 5 attempts.
3. Stationary three-pointers — 5 positions × 5 attempts.
4. One-dribble pull-ups — 3 positions × 5 attempts in each direction.
5. Free throws — 10 attempts.

Prioritize balance, repeatable footwork, a stable shooting position, and a held follow-through. Record makes and attempts for every drill.

### 1-on-1 skills — 15 minutes

- Jab step and drive — 5 repetitions in each direction.
- Hesitation and drive — 5 repetitions in each direction.
- Crossover into a midrange pull-up — 5 repetitions in each direction.
- With a partner: play 6–10 possessions with a maximum of three dribbles.
- Without a partner: attack a cone or chair and shoot or finish within three dribbles.

### Light conditioning — 5 minutes

- Defensive slides: 4 × 20 seconds.
- Rest: 40 seconds between repetitions.
- Do not add maximal sprints this week.

## Saturday — Court and Gym B

**Total time:** 75–85 minutes

Complete basketball before lifting so that gym fatigue does not reduce shooting quality.

### Basketball — 40–45 minutes

1. Close-range form shooting — 15 makes.
2. One-dribble midrange — 20 attempts going left and 20 going right.
3. Three-pointers — 5 positions × 4 attempts.
4. Hesitation into a pull-up — 3 positions × 4 attempts in each direction.
5. Free throws — 10 attempts.

Use approximately 70% of game speed. Prioritize balanced stops and consistent mechanics.

## Week 1 rules

- No intervals at the target 4:00/km pace.
- No maximal sprinting.
- No maximal lifting or training to failure.
- Keep sessions at RPE 6/10 or below, except for the short relaxed strides.
- Reduce a session by 30–50% if soreness changes your running or shooting technique.
- Stop immediately for sharp pain, chest pain, dizziness, or unusual shortness of breath.
- The goal is to complete the week feeling ready for slightly more training.
